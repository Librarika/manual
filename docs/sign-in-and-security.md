# Sign-in & security

**Who this is for:** everyone.

**What you can do here:** find the right page about signing in and account security, and a quick guide for members.

Everything about signing in to Librarika and keeping your account safe is on these pages:

* [Signing in](signing-in.md) (everyone): ways to sign in (password, Google, Apple, your library's SSO), your sign-in settings, confirming it's you and changing your email address.
* [Passwords](passwords.md) (everyone): choosing, changing and resetting your password.
* [Two-step verification](two-step-verification.md) (everyone): turn it on, sign in with a code, recovery codes, a new or lost phone.
* [Sign-in activity & privacy](sign-in-activity.md) (everyone): where you are signed in, signing out a device, emails about your account, and what your library can see.
* [Library sign-in security](library-sign-in-security.md) (library admins): require two-step verification, set password lengths, ask for new passwords.
* [Helping people who can't sign in](helping-people-sign-in.md) (library staff): what to do when a member or colleague is locked out.

Your **Librarika account** belongs to you, not to one library. You use the same account on [librarika.com](https://librarika.com), in every library you belong to and in the mobile app.

## Quick guide for members

* **Sign in:** on your library's **Log in** page, enter your email and your Librarika password, or use Google, Apple or your library's SSO button. See [Ways to sign in](signing-in.md#ways-to-sign-in).
* **Forgot your password:** click `Forgot password?` on the **Log in** page and follow the link in the email. The link works once, for one hour. See [Forgot your password?](passwords.md#forgot-your-password).
* **Choose a good password:** at least 15 characters. A few unrelated words are easy to remember and hard to guess. See [Passwords](passwords.md).
* **Turn on two-step verification:** `My Account` > `Account Settings` > **Two-step verification** > `Turn on`. You need an authenticator app on your phone. Save your recovery codes. See [Two-step verification](two-step-verification.md).
* **Lost your phone:** sign in with one of your recovery codes. No recovery codes? Contact [Librarika support](https://librarika.com/spages/contact); your library can't turn off two-step verification, but it can help you reach support. See [Lost your phone?](two-step-verification.md#lost-your-phone).
