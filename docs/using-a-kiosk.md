# Using a kiosk

**Who this is for:** members of a library that has a Librarika self-checkout kiosk.

**What you can do here:** learn how to sign in with your card and kiosk PIN, borrow, return and renew items at the kiosk, see your holds, set or reset your kiosk PIN, and what to do if the kiosk refuses.

---

## Sign in with your card

1. On the kiosk's start screen (*"Scan your library card to get started"*), scan the barcode on your library card (your member number). You can also type your card number in **Library Card Number** and click `Login`.

	![The kiosk start screen](img/kiosk-start-screen.png)

2. If the library has terms of use, read them and click `I Accept` (or `Decline` to stop). If the library asks, scroll to the end before `I Accept` becomes active. The library may have renamed the button.

You don't need your website password at the kiosk. Some libraries ask for a **kiosk PIN** after you scan your card (see [Enter your PIN](#enter-your-pin)); otherwise your card is enough. Keep your card safe.

You then see your card number, **Items Checked Out**, **Items Overdue** and **Fines Owed** (in your library's currency), and your choices under *"What would you like to do?"*: `Check Out Items`, `Return Items`, `My Items` and, if your library uses holds, `My Holds`.

![The kiosk menu after signing in](img/kiosk-member-menu.png)

This picture is from an older version: when your library uses holds, the menu also has `My Holds`.

## Enter your PIN

If your library asks for a kiosk PIN, the kiosk shows **Enter your PIN** after you scan your card.

![The kiosk asking for a PIN](img/kiosk-pin-enter.png)

1. Type your PIN on the keypad. The kiosk says how many digits it has, for example *"Enter your 6-digit PIN."* `CLEAR` removes what you typed.
2. Click `OK`.
3. If the PIN is right, you see your menu as usual. If not, the kiosk says *"That PIN is not right. Try again."*

Until your PIN is right, the kiosk shows only the last four characters of your card number: not your name, your loans or what you owe. Click `CANCEL` to stop and go back to the start screen. The kiosk forgets your PIN when you log out or it signs you out, so the next person can't use it.

Your kiosk PIN is not your website password. It works only at your library's kiosks, with your card.

**Forgot your PIN?** The kiosk shows the address of your library's PIN page, for example `mylibrary.librarika.com/pin`, with a QR code you can scan with your phone. There you can choose a new one (see [Set or reset your kiosk PIN](#set-or-reset-your-kiosk-pin)). If your library sets PINs only at the desk, the kiosk says *"Forgot your PIN? Please ask the library staff."*

Other screens you may see instead of the keypad (click `DONE` to go back):

* **Too many attempts**: *"Please try again later, or ask the library staff."* After 5 wrong PINs in a row, your card is locked at the kiosks for 15 minutes, then for an hour after each further 5 wrong PINs. After 50 wrong PINs in a row it stays locked until library staff unlock it or you choose a new PIN. You can still borrow at the desk.

	![Too many attempts](img/kiosk-pin-locked.png)

* **Kiosk PIN needed**: *"Please see the library staff to set your kiosk PIN."* You don't have a PIN yet. Ask at the desk, or set one on the library's PIN page if your library allows it.

	![Kiosk PIN needed](img/kiosk-pin-no-pin.png)

* **Choose a new PIN**: your library now uses longer PINs (for example *"Your library now uses 6-digit PINs."*) or asks you to choose a new one. Choose it on the library website (the kiosk shows the address and a QR code) or at the desk.

	![Choose a new PIN](img/kiosk-pin-must-change.png)

* **PIN check not available**: *"PIN check not available. Please ask the library staff."* The kiosk can't check PINs at the moment.

## Set or reset your kiosk PIN

You can choose a new kiosk PIN at the library desk, or yourself with a link sent to your email, if your library allows it.

**At the desk:** staff open your record and turn the screen to you. Type your new PIN twice and they save it. Staff never see your PIN.

**By email:**

1. Go to your library's PIN page: your library's Librarika address followed by `/pin`, for example `mylibrary.librarika.com/pin`. The kiosk shows this address when you get your PIN wrong, and staff can also email you the link.
2. On **Set or reset your kiosk PIN**, type your **Card number** and **Email address** (the one the library has for you), then click `Email me a link`.

	![Set or reset your kiosk PIN](img/kiosk-pin-request-link.png)

3. You see **Check your email**: *"If the card number and email match our records, we've emailed a link. It works once, for 30 minutes."* The page says the same whether or not they match. No email after a few minutes? Check your spam folder, or ask at the library desk.
4. Open the email **Choose your kiosk PIN** and click `Choose my PIN`.
5. On **Choose your kiosk PIN** (it shows the last four characters of your card), type your new PIN in **PIN** and **Type it again**, then click `Save`.

	![Choose your kiosk PIN](img/kiosk-pin-choose.png)

6. You see **PIN saved**: *"Your kiosk PIN is set. Use it with your card at the library's self-service kiosks."*

Your PIN must have the number of digits your library uses (4 or 6), and easy PINs are refused: the same digit repeated, digits counting up or down such as `123456`, repeated pairs such as `121212`, your card number, or PINs many people use. If the link says **Link not valid** (*"This link has expired or was already used. Request a new one."*), click `Request a new link`. Only the newest link works.

Whenever your PIN is set or changed, you get an email *"Your kiosk PIN was changed"* with the date and whether it was done at the desk or with an emailed link. The email never contains your PIN. If it wasn't you, contact the library.

If the page says *"Members cannot set their PIN online at this library. Please ask at the library desk."*, your library sets PINs only at the desk.

## Borrow items

1. Click `Check Out Items`.
2. Scan each item's barcode (or type it in **Item Barcode** and click `Check Out`).
3. Each item shows *"Item … checked out successfully!"* and its due date under **Checked Out Items**.

The due date follows your library's usual time for self-checkout. If you scan an item you already have, the kiosk doesn't lend it again: it shows **Already yours** and *"You already have this item, due …"*. If your library allows renewals, it asks *"Do you want to renew it?"*: click `Renew` to renew it, or `No, keep it` to leave the loan as it is. Nothing is renewed unless you choose `Renew`.

## Return items

1. Click `Return Items`.
2. Scan each item's barcode and click `Return Item` if needed.
3. Each item shows *"Item … returned successfully!"*

If another member has a hold on the item, the kiosk shows **Another member is waiting for this item** and tells you what to do with it, for example *"Please give it to library staff, or leave it where your library asks for held items."* Please don't put it back on the shelf.

## See your items

Click `My Items` to see what you have borrowed, with the due date of each item and how many days are left, or how many days it is overdue. Overdue items are listed first. Click `Back to Menu` to go back.

## Renew items

If your library allows renewals, `My Items` has a `Renew` button on each item, and `Renew all` to renew everything you can.

1. Click `My Items`.
2. Click `Renew` on an item, or `Renew all`.
3. A renewed item shows **Renewed** and *"Now due …"* with its new due date. An item that can't be renewed shows *"Not renewed: …"* with the reason, for example *"Another member is waiting for this item."* or *"This item has been renewed the maximum number of times."* After `Renew all`, the kiosk shows how many items were renewed and how many were not.
4. Click `Done` when you have finished.

The kiosk follows the same renewal rules as [renewing in the member area](member-area.md#renew-your-loans). On an overdue item the kiosk says *"Renewing adds the late fee so far to your account."* Overdue items can be renewed only if your library allows it; otherwise the item has no `Renew` button and the kiosk says *"This item is overdue and can't be renewed here. Please return it or ask at the desk."* An item that it is too early to renew says *"This item can't be renewed yet. Try again closer to its due date."*

If the connection drops while you renew, the kiosk can't tell whether the renewal went through. It says *"We could not confirm whether … was renewed. It may have gone through: please check its due date in My Items, or ask library staff."* Check the due date in `My Items` before you try again.

## See your holds

If your library uses holds, click `My Holds` to see the items you are waiting for. The menu shows how many holds are ready, for example **2 ready**.

* **Ready for pickup**: the item is waiting for you, with the date to collect it by. *"Collect it at the desk."* If your library has more than one branch, the kiosk says which one, for example **Ready for pickup at Main Library**.
* **Waiting**: your place in the queue, for example *"You are 2nd in the queue."*, and the branch to collect it at.
* **Suspended**: a hold you have paused, and until when.

`My Holds` only lists your holds: you can't place, suspend or cancel holds at the kiosk. If you have none, it says *"Holds you place in the catalogue or the app are shown here."*

![My Holds with no holds](img/kiosk-my-holds-empty.png)

To place or manage holds, use the [Member area](member-area.md#my-holds), the Librarika app (see [Get the app & sign in](mobile-app.md)) or ask at the desk.

## Receipts

If the library prints receipts, you get a receipt when you click `Print Receipt`, or by itself when you leave the checkout, return or `My Items` screen if the library prints receipts automatically. A renewal receipt shows each item's new due date. `Print My Items` prints the list of everything you have borrowed. Every receipt ends with the library's contact details when the library includes them. Receipts are printed only; the kiosk doesn't email them.

Your card number is partly hidden on receipts. Depending on the library's setting, receipts show your full name, only your initials, or no name. If the kiosk signs you out because you left it alone, it doesn't print a receipt, so nothing is left in the printer for the next person.

## Finish

Near closing time you may see **Library Closing Soon**: click `I Understand` and finish your loans.

Click `Logout` at the top when you are done. If you leave the kiosk alone, it signs you out by itself: you see **Session Timeout Warning** shortly before; touch the screen or click `Continue Session` to keep going.

## What the kiosk can't do

You can't place holds or pay fees at the kiosk. To place a hold, use the [Member area](member-area.md#my-holds), the Librarika app (see [Get the app & sign in](mobile-app.md)) or ask at the desk. To pay, contact the library. If `Renew` or `My Holds` doesn't appear, your library doesn't offer renewals or holds at its kiosks; ask at the desk.

## If the kiosk refuses

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| *"That PIN is not right. Try again."* | Type your PIN again. If you forgot it, use the library's PIN page shown on the screen, or ask at the desk. The kiosk gives this answer too when it doesn't recognise your card or your membership isn't active. |
| **Too many attempts** | Your card is locked at the kiosks for a while. Try later, choose a new PIN, or ask at the desk. |
| **Kiosk PIN needed** | Ask library staff to set a kiosk PIN for you. |
| *"Your account is blocked. Please see library staff."* | Ask at the desk. Your card may not be recognised, your membership may be inactive, or the kiosk itself may have a problem. |
| *"This card number matches more than one account. Please ask at the desk."* | Your card number belongs to more than one member. Staff need to fix it before you can use the kiosk. |
| *"This item is already checked out to another patron."* (the kiosk says *patron* for member) | The kiosk can't lend this item to you right now. It may be on loan or on hold for someone else, not for loan, or you may have reached your borrowing limit. Take it to the desk. |
| *"Member inactive or invalid."* | Your membership isn't active. Ask at the desk. |
| *"Your account cannot complete this action. Please see library staff."* | Ask at the desk. |
| *"This item could not be checked out. Please try again or ask library staff for help."* | Try once more, then take the item to the desk. |
| *"Item was not checked out."* (when returning) | This item wasn't on loan. Give it to staff. |
| *"Item not found in the system. Please check the barcode."* (when returning) | Scan again, or take the item to the desk. |
| *"This item could not be returned. Please try again or ask library staff for help."* | Try once more, then give the item to staff. |
| **Library Closed** | The kiosk only works during opening hours. |
| **Unable to Connect to Library Server** | The kiosk is offline. Ask at the desk. |
| *"Connection lost. Please check with library staff …"* | The connection dropped during your loan or return. Ask staff to check that the item was recorded. |
| *"We could not confirm whether … was renewed. …"* or *"We could not confirm which items were renewed. …"* | The renewal may have gone through. Check the due dates in `My Items`, or ask staff. |
| *"No items could be renewed. Please ask library staff for help."* | None of your items could be renewed. Ask at the desk, or return the items. |
