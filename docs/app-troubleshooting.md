# Troubleshooting the app

**Who this is for:** members and staff who use the Librarika app.

**What you can do here:** find what a message in the app means and what to do.

---

## Signing in

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| *"Invalid username and password"* | Check your email and password. Forgot it? Tap `Forgot Password?`. |
| *"Too many sign-in attempts. Please wait … seconds; retrying sooner extends the wait."* | Wait for the time shown, then try once. |
| *"Please verify your email address in order to login."* | Open the confirmation email from Librarika and follow its link, then sign in again. Can't find it? Sign in on librarika.com to have it sent again. |
| *"You need to change your password. …"* | Tap `Change password on the website`, choose a new password, then sign in to the app with it. |
| *"Your account needs two-step verification. …"* | Tap `Set up two-step verification` and set it up on the website, then sign in to the app. |
| *"Please update the Librarika app to sign in. Your account uses two-step verification."* | Your app is an older version. Close the app completely and open it again; it updates itself when it starts. If the message stays, update Librarika from the App Store or Google Play. Until then, use your library's website. |
| *"That code is not right. Please try again."* | Type the newest code from your authenticator app. Check that your phone's date and time are set automatically. |
| *"Your sign-in has expired. Please sign in again."* | Sign in again. You see this if you took more than 5 minutes to enter your two-step code, or if this phone was signed out: from **Where you are signed in** on the website, or because your password or two-step verification changed on another device. |
| *"Sign-in was cancelled."* | You declined on the Google or Apple page. Try again and allow Librarika. (If you just close the page, the app returns to Sign In without a message.) |
| *"Please enable the `Login with Google` option from the settings menu."* | Google sign-in is off for your account. Sign in with your email and password, then turn on **Sign in with Google** under `Profile` > **Sign-in methods** (see [Account settings in the app](app-account-settings.md#sign-in-methods-and-sso)), or tick **Enable Google Login** in your settings on the website. |
| *"Google did not confirm that this email address is verified. …"* | Verify the address with Google, or sign in with your password. |
| *"Apple did not share a verified email address. …"* | Sign in with your password, or share your email with Librarika in your Apple ID settings. |

## SSO

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| *"This library does not offer SSO sign-in in the app. Check the address, or sign in with your password."* | Check the library's web address (for example `mylibrary`). If it's right, the library doesn't use SSO: sign in with your password. |
| *"SSO sign-in is not available right now."* | Sign in with your password, Google or Apple. |
| *"This library allows sign-in with its SSO only. …"* | The library allows only its SSO in the app. Sign out, then sign in with the key button and that library's address. |
| *"You signed in with another library's SSO. Sign in with this library's SSO or your password."* | An SSO sign-in opens only the library whose SSO you used. Sign out, then sign in with your password, Google or Apple, or with this library's SSO. |
| *"SSO is not available for this library."* | The library hasn't switched SSO on. Sign in with your password. |

## Libraries and access

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **No Libraries Found** | Your account isn't in any library yet. Accept your library's invitation (see [Member quick start](member-quick-start.md)) or ask to join on librarika.com (`My Libraries` > `Join a library`; the app's hint says *My Account*), then sign in again. |
| The library you want isn't listed | You may have used a different account, or you signed in with a library's SSO (that opens only that library). Tap `Logout` at the bottom of **Select Library**, then sign in with the right account, or with your password, Google or Apple. |
| Member screens instead of staff screens (or the other way) | You tapped the library under the other group. Use `Switch Library` and tap it under **Manage as Librarian** or **Access as Member**. |
| No self-checkout button, holds or requests | Your library hasn't turned them on. Ask your library. |

## Connection and updates

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| *"No internet connection available"* with `Retry` | Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data and tap `Retry`. |
| *"Network Error"* | The app couldn't reach Librarika. Check your connection and try again. |
| **Update Required** | Tap `Update Now` and install the new version from the store. |

## Lost or stolen phone

Sign the phone out from the website: **Where you are signed in** > `Sign out`. See [Lost your phone?](mobile-app.md#lost-your-phone).

Still stuck? Ask your library, or [contact Librarika support](https://librarika.com/spages/contact).
